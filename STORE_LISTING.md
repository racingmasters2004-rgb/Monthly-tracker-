A calm, paper-inspired productivity planner, habit tracker, and expense manager.

## Full Description
**Monthly Planner & Expense Tracker** is a premium, distraction-free productivity system engineered for long-term daily clarity. Designed with a calm paper-and-navy aesthetic, it combines structured personal planning with professional financial tracking — all in one unified, mobile-first app that runs seamlessly on your phone.

### KEY PLANNER FEATURES
- **Calm, High-Clarity Hierarchy:** Organize your life and projects cleanly by Domain → Task → Subtask (e.g., Company, Self, Finance).
- **Weighted Domain Progress:** Automatically computes true accomplishment scores weighted by task priority (High = 2.0x, Medium = 1.5x, Low = 1.0x).
- **Auto-Complete & Celebrations:** Checking the last open subtask completes the parent task with a celebratory gold seal stamp and celebratory audio.
- **Dynamic Eisenhower Matrix:** Automatically maps your tasks into Do Now, Plan It, Delegate, or Drop based on Important/Urgent toggles.
- **2x2 Clickable Dashboard:** Tap to inspect Completed Today, Open Tasks, Stalled Items, and Overall Domain Progress.
- **Weekly Review (Mon–Sun):** Visual planned vs. done progress bars derived automatically from your deadlines.
- **Intelligent Recurring Engine:** Build daily or weekday habit streaks (Mon/Wed/Fri) with zero duplicate instances.
- **Calendar & Ghost Projections:** Full month view with due task counts and future projected recurring instances.
- **Lined-Paper Journal:** Free-write scratchpad with automatic debounced autosave.
- **Human-Readable PDF Reports:** Export clean, professional task summaries grouped by domain.

### BUILT-IN EXPENSE TRACKER
- **Switch Seamlessly:** Access the Expense Tracker directly from the header icon.
- **Income & Expense Tracking:** Track transactions with user-defined categories and icons.
- **Payment Method Distribution:** Real-time visibility into Cash, Card, UPI, and Bank balances.
- **Category Budgets:** Optional monthly spend limits with visual progress meters (calm → amber → red).
- **Recurring Subscriptions:** Auto-scheduled rent, salaries, and recurring bills.
- **Client-Grade PDF Financial Statements:** Download bank-statement-style PDFs with executive KPI summaries and transaction ledgers.

## App Store Privacy Labels
- **Data Used to Track You:** None.
- **Data Linked to You:** User profile name, preferences (if cloud account enabled).
- **Data Not Linked to You:** Diagnostics, anonymous usage metrics (opt-out).
- **Local Data Storage:** All data persists locally in high-performance encrypted browser storage or on-device storage.
